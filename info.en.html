The map of Berlin 1:5000 (K5) from more than 30 years, from 1995 until today, from the Geoportal Berlin. Choose a year and see how the city has changed. Split the screen to compare several years – all panes move and zoom together.

The K5 is the official city map of Berlin and shows every building, every street and every green space. For example, you can see how the centre of the city was rebuilt after the fall of the Wall or how your neighbourhood has changed over the last decades.

The site was created in collaboration with Volker Stoi from blnmap.de.

A project of the OK Lab Berlin. The tiles are served by tiles.codefor.de and the source code is on GitHub: stadtplan.berlin.codefor.de and @jochenklar/maps. Aerial images are on luftbilder.berlin.codefor.de and more maps on maps.berlin.codefor.de.
